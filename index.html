Unsupervised Machines

True AI stories, told by robots.

Unsupervised Machines is a YouTube channel that explains real stories about artificial intelligence going wrong (and right) as short animated films.

▶ Watch on YouTube

The channel uses a small private tool, the Unsupervised Machines uploader, to upload its own finished videos to its own YouTube channel and to save review copies to the owner's own Google Drive.
